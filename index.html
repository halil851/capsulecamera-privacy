Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 13, 2026

At CapsuleCamera, we value your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use our app.

1. Photos and Media Storage

Your photos and videos are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your personal media, and we do not upload them to any servers.

2. Information Collection via AdMob

To keep this app free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to show relevant ads to you:

  • Device Identifiers: Such as IDFA (on iOS) or Advertising ID.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with ads within the app.
  • Approximate Location: Used to show geographically relevant ads.

For more details on how Google handles your data, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: To capture photos for your private albums.
  • Photo Library: To import/save photos to your device.
  • Tracking (iOS): If you give permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, we use your device's advertising identifier to provide personalized ads.

4. Data Retention and Security

Personal media stays on your device. Ad-related data is handled by Google’s secure infrastructure according to their retention policies.

5. Your Choices (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have specific rights regarding your data. You can manage your ad personalization preferences within your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
